Corporate Leave Policies & Rules

Advance Notice: Planned leaves should be applied at least 2 business days in advance.
Overlapping Rule: The system automatically blocks submission of dates that overlap with an existing pending or approved request.
Approval Workflow: Requests submitted by team members are routed to the designated reporting manager for review.
Date Integrity: End date cannot precede the start date, and all dates are validated on both client and server layers.
